## Evidence

tee-bench exp 46c, gpu31 bare metal, TP2, base prod argv, 1.0 conv/s, 600 s, two lane-swapped rounds vs paired v7.

Q8-only (8K chunks) vs v7:
- completed requests +4.0%;
- queue-full rejections -14%;
- output tok/s +8.2%;
- TTFT p50 -27%, TTFT p95 -14%;
- ITL p95 -8%;
- GSM8K-100 0.99;
- smoke gate: no fatals, no new failures.

Rejected: Q8 with 16K chunks for base (ITL p95 +43%, TTFT p50 +27%). This is why the canary keeps 8192.

**Confound:** v10 also carries v8's zero-sync KDA patch (#352). It is neutral on bare metal and has been in prod on gpu03 r4 since 10-12. The exp 46c comparison therefore includes the KDA patch, not the Q8 patch alone.

## Rules

- **At most 2 base replicas down fleet-wide** (r4 counts as one). Check that every other base replica is up first (`/backends/list` and `docker/ps` on gpu03 and gpu04). No other replica may be down or recreating when r4 is recreated.
- **Never kill a running compose/up.** An interrupted apply leaves the replica created but not started. Wait for it to finish, or re-run the scoped `up`.
- **gpu-manager cuts the stream at 5 min.** After the `up` call, check `docker/ps` on gpu04. If r4 is absent or still in `Created`, re-send the scoped `up`. Do not start a second apply while one is running.
- **Dry run first.** The plan must recreate exactly `model-sg-glm53-w4afp8-tp2-r4` and nothing else. Any other service in the plan stops the deploy.
- **No `compose/down`.** Ever, for this canary.
- **Tell the team (Henry) before the deploy**, with the replica and time.
- The long tier is NOT part of this canary. A long-tier step comes only after the base read, and again one replica at a time (`docs/glm53-long-q8-canary.md`).

## Deploy (compose-manager, gpu04 only)

`POST /compose/up` with `tag` = the merged tag, `file: prod/GLM-5.3-Flash-SGL-TP2x4-W4AFP8-Q8Canary.yaml`, `services: ["model-sg-glm53-w4afp8-tp2-r4"]`, the host's existing full `env` map, `force_recreate: false`.
1. `dry_run: true` first. The plan must recreate exactly that one service.
2. Then `dry_run: false`. Poll `docker/ps` until r4 shows a NEW container `Up N minutes` on the v10 image. If it stays absent for over a minute, re-send the real `up`.
3. Ready = `/health` 200, `/backends/list` shows r4 ready, and it serves traffic.

Cold start under CC is about 20 minutes.
